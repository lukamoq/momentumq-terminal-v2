ALPHA & BENCHMARK METRICS

Relative alpha is calculated as Realized Stock Return − Realized SPY Return over the identical forecast holding window. A buy recommendation on a stock that rose 10% while SPY rose 25% represents negative alpha (-15%).

EQUAL-WEIGHT S&P 500 (RSP)

The Invesco S&P 500 Equal Weight ETF (RSP) is tracked as the benchmark for "broadening out" and cyclical rotation calls. Repeated calls predicting equal-weight outperformance consistently lagged the cap-weighted S&P 500.

EXACT SPOT AT PUBLICATION

Entry spots are locked to the closing price on or immediately preceding report publication date. Target percentage returns are evaluated against actual subsequent market highs and horizon realizations.
