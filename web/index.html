SPY × 10 INDEX PROXY

Due to licensing restrictions on direct cash index feeds, the S&P 500 index level is proxied as SPY closing price × 10.0 from Massive daily bars. The cash-to-proxy tracking error is <0.2%, well inside the 2.0% directional indifference band.

2% DIRECTION BAND & AUGUST TARGET

Direction derives strictly as implied = (target / spot) - 1. Implied > +2% is bullish; < -2% is bearish. JPM's 10 Aug 2026 raise to 8,000 against spot ~7,730 implies +3.49% upside, which derives as bullish, not neutral.

OVERLAPPING WINDOW AUTOCORRELATION

Multi-horizon evaluations and daily stance-days utilize overlapping time windows. These observations are autocorrelated and presented for descriptive empirical tracking, not independent hypothesis tests.
